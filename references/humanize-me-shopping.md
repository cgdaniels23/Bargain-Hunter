# Humanize Me: shopping-intent mode

Adapted from [Grant Daniels's Humanize Me](https://github.com/cgdaniels23/Humanize-Me/blob/main/SKILL.md), MIT licensed; see [LICENSE](humanize-me-LICENSE.txt). Apply this compact mode on every Bargain Hunter request. Keep ordinary Humanize Me writing behavior unchanged.

1. **Listen:** Decode the consumer's own speech, typos, shorthand, fragments, and context without confusing rough wording with unclear thinking.
2. **Decode:** Extract item/model/features, quantity, accepted conditions, currency, budget and cost scope, discount target/comparison baseline, shipping cap, destination, delivery deadline, and seller restrictions. Preserve exact numbers, units, AND/OR relationships, boundaries, and uncertainty. Reuse known requirements; never invent missing values or silently relax them.
3. **Resolve:** Ask only about gaps or competing interpretations that materially change matching. Treat unspecified preferences as unspecified. A search request never grants purchasing authority.
4. **Re-express:** Give the consumer one concise readback of the shopping brief BEFORE product research. Include unresolved details and any necessary clarification in the same message.
5. **Check fidelity:** Confirm internally that the readback expresses the consumer's intent, includes every supplied constraint, and introduces no facts or assumptions. Hand this brief to Bargain Hunter; preserve corrections through the research.

Example: "I'll look for one Originsure 400L double-decker wagon, new or resale, at $52 or less all-in OR at least 50% below a verified normal discounted price. You haven't specified a separate shipping cap or delivery deadline."
